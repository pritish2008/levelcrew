Questions

Do we need to pay for Claude?

No. We build the training for the plan you have, Free included. Some parts of Claude, like Research and Cowork, come only with a paid plan.

What do my staff need?

A phone or a computer, and a Claude account.

Is our data safe?

You tell us what must never go into AI, and the training sticks to it. We never see your team’s chats with Claude.

What does LevelCrew cost?

It depends on how many teams you want trained. We tell you on the call, before you agree to anything.
